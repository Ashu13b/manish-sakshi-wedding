⚖️ Legal, Licensing & DPDP Privacy
🔒 DPDP Act 2023 & Guest Privacy COMPLIANT

• Data Fiduciary: The host wedding family acts as the Data Fiduciary. WeddingCraft provides technical processing only.

• Purpose Limitation: Guest RSVP submissions are sent directly to the host family's WhatsApp. Names, phone numbers, and dietary preferences are collected solely for ceremony coordination and seating.

• Zero Server Storage: This website is a static page hosted on Cloudflare Pages. RSVP data is transmitted to the host family via WhatsApp — WeddingCraft does not store or have access to guest submissions. No tracking cookies, no ad profiling.

• Right to Erasure: Guests may request removal of their RSVP data by contacting the wedding host family directly.
